# P7: QUASAR under Look-Locker, Look-Locker combinations, and 3D gradient-echo trains — implementation plan

**Goal:** implement the P7 addendum (`docs/specs/2026-10-06-p7-quasar-ll-3d-design.md`) across
`mrsim-acq` and `aslscan`, with every existing output byte-identical. It has three parts:

- **Part A:** the P4 parts under 2D Look-Locker readouts:
  - the arterial (QUASAR) compartment;
  - per-readout crushing;
  - exchange;
  - bolus-position suppression.
- **Part B:** Look-Locker × multi-TE and Look-Locker × Hadamard.
- **Part C:** a 3D gradient-echo stack-of-EPI readout. On it, `aslscan` builds:
  - single-readout 3D gradient-echo ASL;
  - 3D Look-Locker;
  - multi-TE 3D;
  - their combinations with Hadamard and the Part A components.

  Label is depleted from its slab entry.

**Architecture.** The baseline comes first: the aslscan regression gate gains P6 cases (it has
none) and is re-pointed at `p6-complete`. It must be shown to pass and to detect a change before
any P7 change. Then the three parts follow as milestones, in order of increasing new machinery:

- **Milestone A** (Part A) is new closed-form kinetics plus lifted refusals, on P6's existing
  Look-Locker series.
- **Milestone B** (Part B) is plumbing: the echo call and per-readout decoding.
- **Milestone C** (Part C) is the large one.
  - `mrsim-acq` first: the readout types, the timing check, and the new entry point with volumes
    built on demand.
  - Then `aslscan`: protocol inputs; the excitation-train timeline, tissue groups and M0;
    slab-entry depletion; the blood interpolation; and the series. It is built up from the
    single-readout case to 3D Look-Locker, then multi-TE and Hadamard.

Each milestone ends with its acceptance task and the gates. The slab-entry shift (`Delta`) is
built into Milestone A's kinetics from the start, with `Delta = 0` as P6's read, so Milestone C
adds no second form.

## Milestone A: the P4 parts under Look-Locker

### Task 1: `aslscan` — the arrival-window kinetics

All new functions; `delta_m_arrival` and `delta_m_read` are not edited.

- **`delta_m_arrival_iv(k, f, dt, t1t, m0, t, u1, u2, tau_ex)`**: `delta_m_arrival` with `T1''`.
  - The PASL branch is in the combined-exponent form `exp(kk (t - u_hi)) expm1(kk (u_hi - u_lo))`,
    with the window cut as `delta_m_arrival` does.
  - It is clamped to `[0, delta_m_arrival(same window)]`.
  - (P)CASL is the residue difference with `T1''`.
  - It keeps the guards of `delta_m_iv` (`T1''` from `t1pp`).
- **`delta_m_arrival_sub(.., t, u1, u2, a, b)`** and **`delta_m_arrival_iv_sub`** apply P4's
  shifts, as `delta_m_sub` does:
  - (P)CASL: `tau = b - a`, evaluated at `t - a`, with the window `[u1 - a, u2 - a)`;
  - PASL: `dt + a`, `tau = b - a`, with the window unshifted;
  - `a == 0 && b == tau` calls the unshifted function.
- **`delta_m_read_parts(k, f, dt, t1t, m0, e, flips, subs: &[(a, b, F)], tau_ex: Option<f64>,
  delta: f64) -> ReadParts { iv, ev }`**: the depletion sum with windows
  `[e_w + delta, e_{w+1} + delta)`.
  - The first window starts at 0. Every window is cut at `t = e_n`.
  - Without exchange everything is `iv`, so `ev = 0`: P4's "all label intravascular".
  - With `subs` a single `(0, tau, 1)`, `tau_ex` `None` and `delta = 0`, it equals `delta_m_read`.
    That equality is a test, not a substitution; the series calls `delta_m_read` itself in that
    case (Task 4).
- **`delta_m_read_all(.., e, flips, subs, tau_ex, delta) -> Vec<ReadParts>`**: the read at
  **every** excitation of a cycle in O(n) per voxel, not O(n²). This is the form Tasks 4, 8 and
  14-16 call; `delta_m_read_parts` is its tested reference.
  - Each full window's arrival term splits into a factor of `t` times a factor of the window:
    - (P)CASL: `exp(-t/T1')` (`T1''` for the intravascular part) times
      `exp(u_hi/T1') - exp(u_lo/T1')`;
    - PASL: `exp(kk t)` times the window's `exp(-kk u)` difference.
  - So the depletion-weighted sum of full windows satisfies `S_{n+1} = cos(a_n) S_n + g_n`.
  - With the shift `delta`, at most one window is cut at `t = e_n`. Its term is evaluated
    directly and added. Windows entirely after `t` contribute zero.
  - **Overflow.** The window factor `exp(u/T1')` overflows over long series clocks. The recursion
    is carried relative to the previous excitation: `S` is rescaled by
    `exp(-(e_{n+1} - e_n)/T1')`, or by `exp(kk (e_{n+1} - e_n))` for PASL, at each step, so
    every exponent is a difference within one cycle.
  - The PASL intravascular branch uses the combined-exponent form inside each `g_n`.
- **`arterial_read(k, abv, aatt, m0, e_n, earlier: &[(e_m, flip_m)], delta_a, factor) -> f64`**:
  `arterial_dm(e_n)` times the `cos` of every earlier excitation in `[e_n - delta_a, e_n)`, times
  `factor` (survival and the parcel's bolus-position factor). `delta_a = 0` is Part A's fresh
  term.

Tests:

- `tau_ex → ∞` (1e9 s): `delta_m_arrival_iv` equals `delta_m_arrival` to `1e-12` relative.
- Windows partitioning `[0, t)` sum to `delta_m_iv` to `1e-12`, for PCASL and PASL.
  - This includes a PASL case at `tau_ex = 0.01` s, where the naive form is non-finite. The test
    computes the naive expression inline and asserts `!is_finite()`.
- Per-window clamp: no window's intravascular part exceeds its whole, and none is negative.
- `delta_m_arrival_sub` over a sub-bolus partition sums to `delta_m_arrival`. Over both
  partitions it sums to `delta_m`.
- `delta_m_read_parts` reductions to `delta_m_read`, to `1e-12`:
  - `delta = 0`;
  - one full sub-bolus;
  - no exchange.
- `delta > 0` shifts: a hand case where one parcel's slab entry precedes an excitation that its
  arrival follows.
- `delta_m_read_all`:
  - equals `delta_m_read_parts` at every excitation, to `1e-12` relative;
  - for PCASL and PASL, with and without exchange, sub-boli and `delta > 0`;
  - stability: a 64-excitation train starting at `t = 30 s` on the series clock stays finite and
    matches;
  - a timing test on 10⁵ voxels × 48 excitations shows the linear cost, and the time is recorded
    in Measurements.
- `arterial_read`:
  - `delta_a = 0` equals `sin`-free `arterial_dm`;
  - two excitations in the window give `cos(a1) cos(a2)`;
  - an excitation exactly at `e_n - delta_a` counts, and one at `e_n` does not.

### Task 2: `aslscan` — the parcel reference

`kinetic/parcel_ref.rs` (`#[cfg(test)]`) is a brute-force midpoint quadrature over labeling
parcels. It shares no code with Task 1 beyond `Kinetic`. Each parcel:

- is labeled at `l` ((P)CASL) or delivered at `t'` (PASL);
- enters the slab at the plug-flow entry time (`d`; `"arrival"` means at arrival);
- arrives at `l + ATT`;
- decays with `T1b` before arrival and with `T1'` after;
- is still intravascular with probability `exp(-s/tau_ex)`;
- takes the factor of every suppression pulse it is inside the region for (P4 part D's table);
- is multiplied by `cos(a_m)` at each excitation after its slab entry and before the read.

The arterial parcel is evaluated directly at `e_n - aATT`.

Tests: the reference itself against closed forms in the trivial limits:

- no excitations: `delta_m` to `1e-6`;
- no exchange: `iv = total`;
- with P4 part D: P4's `0.0821045` worked factor.

Then the Task 1 functions against the reference, to `1e-6` relative:

- for PCASL and PASL;
- with exchange, bolus-position `"slab"` (numeric `d`) and `"global"`;
- at `Delta = 0` and `Delta > 0`;
- with arrival before, during and after the readouts;
- the arterial term with `delta_a = 0` and `delta_a > 0`.

The quadrature's own convergence is shown at two resolutions: their difference is below `1e-7`.

- [ ] Implement and test; commit (`aslscan`): `test: parcel reference for depleted reads`.

### Task 7: `aslscan` — Look-Locker × Hadamard: protocol and schedule

- **The Look-Locker cycles come from the Hadamard structure, not from the arrays.**
  `look_locker_spec` finds cycles as runs of rows with increasing delay (`protocol.rs:1293-1302`).
  Under Hadamard the context is readout-major decoded rows, and within one readout's block the
  effective delay decreases with `j`. So every row would become its own cycle, and the
  `readouts_per_cycle` check (`:1304-1312`) would refuse every valid protocol.
  - With `[hadamard]` on, `look_locker_spec` skips the run rule. `hadamard_spec` builds the
    cycles: `H` Look-Locker cycles per encoding cycle, one per encoding row, with `M` readouts
    each.
  - `readouts_per_cycle` is required under Hadamard and gives `M`. It must be one value for the
    series, and the decoded row count per encoding cycle must be `(H - 1) M`.
- **`hadamard_spec` under Look-Locker** (`protocol.rs:1096`):
  - The context lists **decoded** rows: `(H - 1) M` `deltam` rows per encoding cycle,
    readout-major, with `m0scan` rows only between encoding cycles.
  - Row `(j, n)`'s delay must be `PLD_n` plus the durations of the sub-boli after `j`, P6's check
    at `protocol.rs:1156-1165` applied per readout. `PLD_n` must strictly increase.
  - `control` and `label` rows stay refused.
- **Per-row arrays are per decoded row.** `FlipAngle`, `VascularCrushingVENC` and the
  suppression sets (keyed by delay) each have one entry per decoded row `(j, n)`, so they are
  mapped to readouts as follows.
  - **`FlipAngle` and VENC:** they must agree across `j` for each `n`. Readout `n` takes that
    value. A disagreement is refused, naming `(j, n)` and the first value.
  - **VENC per encoding cycle:** P6 requires one VENC per encoding cycle (`:1196-1201`). Under
    Look-Locker that rule becomes **one VENC per readout index within an encoding cycle**, which
    is what per-readout crushing needs. Without Look-Locker, P6's rule is unchanged.
  - **Suppression:** one pulse set per encoding cycle, taken from the rows of readout 1 (keyed by
    their delays, `PLD_1` plus the later sub-boli). The other readouts' rows must map to the same
    set, or the error names the readout.
  - **Pulse limit:** P6 refuses a pulse at or after `tau_tot + pld_n` (`:1181-1186`), where
    `pld_n` is the last sub-bolus's delay. Under Look-Locker `pld_n` is `PLD_1`, the first
    readout: pulses precede the first excitation (P6 part B).
- **Schedule** (`schedule.rs`):
  - an encoding cycle is `H` Look-Locker cycles, one per encoding row;
  - each Look-Locker cycle is one preparation (2D) and gives `M` raw volumes;
  - `RawVolume` gains `readout: usize`;
  - `Output::Decoded` gains `readout`;
  - `TotalAcquiredPairs` remains `cycles.len()`, the encoding cycles (`bids.rs:597`).

Tests:

- context acceptance, including a context whose decreasing within-block delays would have split
  under the run rule;
- each refusal:
  - a `label` row;
  - a wrong effective delay, naming `(j, n)`;

### Task 10: `mrsim-acq` — `simulate_acquisition_3d_ge`

- `GeVolume { images, weights, shot_images }` and the entry point of the addendum, with
  `volume: &(dyn Fn(usize) -> GeVolume + Sync)`.
- Per volume `g`, the worker:
  1. calls `volume(g)` and asserts the compartment count;
  2. **per echo `e`**, runs compartment `c`'s per-slice 2D forward with
     `Acquisition { echo: EchoFormation::Gradient, t_echo: TE_e, do_relaxation: false, .. }`.
     - The fieldmap phase is then the existing code's, not a line weight. The static
       `2π fmap TE_e` joins the object phase when `echo == Gradient` and distortions are on,
       whatever `do_relaxation` is (`kspace.rs:636-641`). The phase accrued during the block
       comes from the line times `t`, as on the GRASE path.
     - So there is one forward per echo per compartment: `E` times a one-echo call's forward
       cost. Measurements record it.
  3. forms partition `p`'s in-plane k-space as `sum_c w[(p, sy, c)] ×` the z-DFT row `p` of that
     forward, times the **decay only**, `exp(-(TE_e + t)(1/T2_c + 1/T2'_c))`.
     - In class mode the decay is a line weight.
     - In voxel mode it is applied per voxel inside the forward, as GRASE's `Mode::Voxel` does
       with maps.
     - The fieldmap is never a line weight.
  4. adds noise and spikes per partition with `SEED_SALT_3D` and the receiver seed
     `seed ^ echo_salt(e)`. The prepared-shot phase uses the unsalted seed.
  5. reconstructs as `simulate_acquisition_3d_complex` does: inverse z-DFT, then coil
     combination.
  6. drops the `GeVolume`.
- **Helpers shared with GRASE** (the z-kernel, per-partition noise and spikes, coil
  reconstruction) are moved into private functions that both paths call. The moves are verbatim,
  in the same order. If a helper cannot be moved without touching GRASE's arithmetic, it is
  duplicated instead and the duplication noted.
- **Refusals** (assertions with messages, as on the 3D path): partial Fourier below 1, the eddy
  model, and multiband.

Tests (the addendum's mrsim-acq list):

- **Direct-forward reference.** With one shot, no noise, uniform relaxation and constant weights,
  the result equals the per-partition 2D EPI forward of the z-DFT of the object, with 2D
  gradient-echo decay. It holds to the `f32` bound under both feature sets.
  - It runs with a **nonzero, spatially varying fieldmap** (a linear gradient plus a bump) and
    two echo times, so a fieldmap phase applied as a line weight, or a missing static phase,
    fails it.
  - A negative control applies `exp(i 2π fmap_mean TE)` as a line weight in place of the
    per-voxel phase and must fail.
- **Through-plane blur.** With weights varying across `p`, the result equals
  `inverse z-DFT(w_p × z-DFT(object))`, computed separately.
- **Echoes.** Echo 0 of a two-echo call equals the one-echo call bit for bit. Echo 1 with noise
  off equals a one-echo call at `TE_1`. The noise residuals are uncorrelated within `3/sqrt(n)`,
  and the shot phase is shared, with P6's negative control.
- **Volumes on demand.** With noise and spikes on, the result equals the same call fed from
  precomputed images, under one thread and under many, bit for bit. A counter shows one call per
  volume. The wrong compartment count panics.
- **Refusals.** Each refusal fires.
- **Byte identity.** The golden record and the GRASE and spiral pins are unchanged.
